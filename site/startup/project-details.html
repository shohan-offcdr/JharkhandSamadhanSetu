Technical Architecture Summary

The proposed system deploys clamp-on piezoelectric hydrophones paired with non-intrusive 865 MHz LoRaWAN telemetry nodes. Sensor microcontrollers execute high-frequency cross-correlation and Fast Fourier Transform (FFT) spectral analysis to detect specific acoustic hiss anomalies generated by pressurised fluid cavitation at pipeline fissures.
