IoT Acoustic Water Leak Telemetry System (Kanke Block)

Autonomous acoustic sensors deployed at valve nodes across Kanke reservoir distribution mains to detect subsurface fractures, minimize non-revenue water leakage, and trigger automatic municipal redressing tickets.
